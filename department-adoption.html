Department AI adoption map

AI adoption has to be designed department by department.

A useful AI rollout is not a single tool decision. It is a connected operating model across leadership, money, trust, market, operations, product, engineering and data work.

Recommendations

Practical adoption moves by department.

The goal is not to automate everything. The goal is to prepare better work, route it to the right human, keep records and learn from exceptions.

Leadership Layer
  • Create an AI decision cockpit for active initiatives, risks and owners.
  • Define what agents report upward and when they escalate.
  • Keep decision logs for strategic assumptions and approvals.
Money Layer
  • Start with invoice, vendor, forecast or procurement preparation.
  • Require human approval before commitments or money movement.
  • Track spend, model cost, exceptions and audit evidence.
People & Trust Layer
  • Map employee data boundaries before using AI in HR or legal work.
  • Create policy review and compliance evidence workflows.
  • Train teams on what AI may suggest and what humans must decide.
Market Layer
  • Use AI for research, segmentation, proposal drafts and CRM hygiene.
  • Put brand, legal and accuracy review before external messaging.
  • Measure quality by pipeline movement, not content volume alone.
Operating Layer
  • Use agents to triage tickets, monitor queues and prepare exceptions.
  • Define SLA, incident and escalation visibility.
  • Start with slow or repetitive workflows where review is easy.
Build & Intelligence Layer
  • Coordinate coding, data, product and research agents with evaluation gates.
  • Reuse knowledge across workflows instead of repeating context.
  • Govern tool access, runtime permissions and experiment records.
