AI distribution changed. The next adoption question is under what authority a system should be allowed to act.

Adoption signalModels are no longer the only adoption question. Authority, permission boundaries and accountable escalation become the bottleneck.

Adoption signalAdoption pressure increases when teams ask not what AI can write, but what AI can safely do.
